# Muhammad Rafiq

I build software that makes AI systems more reliable, understandable, and useful.

I'm an Economics student at the University of Michigan interested in AI workflow execution, Apple platforms, automation, smart-home technology, and developer tools.

## Featured Projects

### [Ghost](https://github.com/mohabbis/ghost)

A trust layer for AI agents that adds human approval, verification, replay safety, and auditability to automated workflows.

Ghost is my clearest example of how I approach systems design: powerful automation should also be constrained, observable, and recoverable.

### [Lumen](https://github.com/mohabbis/Lumen)

A native iOS smart-home companion focused on contextual automation, routines, and understandable HomeKit experiences.

### [FlipDeck](https://github.com/mohabbis/flipdeck)

A Mac and Flipper Zero toolkit that turns a physical device into a configurable command deck for desktop workflows.

## Engineering Perspective

I enjoy building systems where reliability matters more than novelty. My work focuses on human-in-the-loop automation, operational software, and interfaces that make complicated systems understandable.

I prefer explicit permissions, verifiable outcomes, useful failure states, and tools that solve real workflow problems rather than decorative AI wrappers.
